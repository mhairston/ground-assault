GROUND ASSAULT — v2 (chosen direction, revised)

Persistent Defender-style world. Roamers hunt humanoids, run bombing raids on your buildings, and shoot at you — no Invaders formations. Walk, climb ladders that carry you up onto rooftops, explore interiors, then board your ship and take to the air — land it anywhere you could walk, ground or rooftop. Gunfire no longer scars buildings, and by default your ship flies safely past them too. Collect superbombs and fire-suppressant tanks from rooftops to fight back and repair any bomb damage.
