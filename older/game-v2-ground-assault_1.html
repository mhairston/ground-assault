GROUND ASSAULT — v2 (chosen direction, revised)

Persistent Defender-style world. Roamers hunt humanoids, run bombing raids on your buildings, and shoot at you — no Invaders formations. Walk, climb ladders that carry you up onto rooftops, explore interiors, then board your ship and take to the air. Your own fire can damage (and destroy) buildings, so watch where you shoot.
